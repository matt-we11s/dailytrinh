Her story

Trinh moved from Vietnam to Missouri and missed the vegetables she grew up with. She couldn’t buy them here, so she grew them herself. Trinh cooks what they harvest.

She films all of it, from seed to supper.

How she grows

She grows clean, home-grown food. She keeps insects, squirrels, and rabbits out with netting, and she bags the fruit to guard it from pests.

Who watches

Trinh posted her first vlog on Aug. 1, 2026. On Aug. 20 the page had about 1.3k followers. On Oct. 26 it passed 100k.

In the last 28 days (Sept. 27 to Oct. 24, 2026), her videos drew 13.9M views from 4.85M unique viewers, who watched for 124,328 hours. Her most-watched video, Vlog 35, shows a harvest of watermelon, cantaloupe, apples, and vegetables for her mother-in-law. It has 12.5M views.

Most of her followers live in Vietnam: 91.65% of those with a known country. Another 2.75% live in the US.
